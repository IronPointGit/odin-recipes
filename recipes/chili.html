Chili

A hearty meal for weeknights with warm spices

Ingredients

  • Tomatoes
  • Tomato puree
  • Ground Beef
  • Broth
  • Onions
  • Garlic
  • Olive Oil
  • Kidney beans

Directions

  1. Heat oil in a skillet and add the ground beef and onions and garlic.
  2. Transfer beef to slow cooker, stir in broth, diced tomatoes and tomato puree
  3. cook on low for 4 to 6 hours.
  4. serveon its own or over rice and enjoy
